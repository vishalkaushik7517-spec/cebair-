01 · Classical evidence

Selected Timira descriptions

The Sanskrit is presented as the primary textual layer. Modern interpretation is intentionally kept separate.

Uttara Tantra 7/11
ऊर्ध्वं पश्यति नाधस्तात्तृतीयं पटलं गते ।
महान्त्यपि च रूपाणि च्छादितानीव वाससा ॥

ūrdhvaṃ paśyati nādhastāt tṛtīyaṃ paṭalaṃ gate | mahānty api ca rūpāṇi cchāditānīva vāsasā ||

Working meaning: altered visual perception in relation to direction and an obscured/covered appearance of forms.
Uttara Tantra 7/12
कर्णनासाक्षियुक्तानि विपरीतानि वीक्षते ।
यथादोषं च रज्येत दृष्टिर्दोषे बलीयसि ॥

karṇa-nāsākṣi-yuktāni viparītāni vīkṣate | yathādoṣaṃ ca rajyeta dṛṣṭir doṣe baliyasi ||

Working meaning: abnormal/altered visual perception associated with the described doṣic disturbance.
Uttara Tantra 7/13
अधःस्थिते समीपस्थं दूरस्थं चोपरिस्थिते ।
पार्श्वस्थिते तथा दोषे पार्श्वस्थानि न पश्यति ॥

adhaḥsthite samīpasthaṃ dūrasthaṃ coparisthite | pārśvasthite tathā doṣe pārśvasthāni na paśyati ||

Working meaning: the described disturbance varies with location and includes failure to perceive objects in particular spatial regions.
Uttara Tantra 7/14
समन्ततः स्थिते दोषे सङ्कुलानीव पश्यति ।
दृष्टिमध्यगते दोषे स एकं मन्यते द्विधा ॥

samantataḥ sthite doṣe saṅkulānīva paśyati | dṛṣṭimadhyagate doṣe sa ekaṃ manyate dvidhā ||

Key phrase: sa ekaṃ manyate dvidhā — one object is perceived as two.
Uttara Tantra 7/15
द्विधास्थिते त्रिधा पश्येद्बहुधा चानवस्थिते ।

dvidhāsthite tridhā paśyed bahudhā cānavasthite |

Key observation: perception of three or multiple forms in the stated circumstances.

Textual note: the online source consulted presents the remainder with omissions; CEBAIR should verify the complete verse against a selected standard edition before final publication.

Textual caution: Some online translations use “night blindness” for Timira. CEBAIR does not adopt that as an automatic modern diagnosis.

03 · Evidence library

What published studies show

Clinical studies have investigated Timira in relation to myopia/refractive error. They support a researchable association, not automatic diagnostic identity.

Clinical study · 2010

Akshi Tarpana ± Nasya

An open randomized study enrolled 41 patients, with 30 completing, and evaluated visual acuity and clinical refraction in Timira with special reference to myopia.

Interpretation: clinically investigated correlation; small study and open design.

PubMed · PMID 22048542
Clinical study · 2011

Jeevantyadi Ghrita

A study enrolled 54 patients (108 eyes) with myopia ≥ −6 D and compared Akshi-Tarpana with Jeevantyadi Ghrita versus plain Go Ghrita.

Interpretation: useful for hypothesis generation, not proof that all Timira represents myopia.

PubMed · PMID 22661851
Clinical study · 2012

Trataka / Eye exercises

A study of 66 patients included refractive-error groups under an Uttana Timira framework; subjective outcomes were more favorable than objective outcomes.

CEBAIR lesson: subjective improvement and objective ophthalmic change must be separated.

PubMed · PMID 23723673
04 · Evidence grading

CEBAIR evidence language

A · Direct validation

Prospective clinical evidence with operationalized classical and modern phenotypes.

B · Strong correspondence

Consistent correspondence supported by multiple high-quality sources.

C · Plausible correlation

Reasonable relationship supported by limited or indirect evidence.

D · Research hypothesis

Interesting possibility requiring formal clinical validation.

05 · Research lab

Turn a classical description into a testable question.

Primary CEBAIR hypothesis

Do defined classical Timira phenotypes demonstrate reproducible associations with specific modern ophthalmological phenotypes?

Study design idea

Define classical phenotypes prospectively, record visual acuity, refraction and standardized ocular examination, then test associations with predefined modern phenotypes.

AI role

Assist with literature retrieval, Sanskrit terminology extraction, phenotype normalization and research-gap discovery with human verification.

Validation principle

A correlation should be independently tested before being presented as clinical equivalence.

References & source policy

Traceable research.

Each future CEBAIR record will include source, study design, population, intervention/exposure, outcomes, limitations and DOI/PMID where available.
