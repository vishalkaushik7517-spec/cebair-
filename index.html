Our mission

From classical knowledge to testable science.

CEBAIR does not assume that a classical disease term is automatically identical to a modern diagnosis. We separate textual evidence, interpretation, modern phenotype and clinical validation.

01 · CLASSICAL

Samhita Explorer

Śloka, reference, transliteration, terminology and meaning with source-critical notes.

02 · EVIDENCE

Evidence Library

Clinical trials, systematic reviews, pharmacology, safety and formulation research.

03 · TRANSLATION

Ayurveda × Modern Medicine

Map clinical phenotypes to modern concepts without forcing one-to-one equivalence.

04 · CLINICAL

Clinical Research

Case phenotyping, outcomes, protocols, databases and reproducible research workflows.

05 · PHARMACY

Pharmacy Research

Quality control, standardization, formulation science, pharmacovigilance and interactions.

06 · AI

AI + Ayurveda

NLP, literature mining, terminology extraction, research assistance and data analysis.

Responsible AI

AI should accelerate research—not replace judgment.

CEBAIR will use AI to assist researchers with retrieval, extraction, organization and analysis while keeping source verification, privacy, clinical responsibility and human review central.

Literature AI

Search, summarize, compare and organize published evidence with traceable sources.

Samhita NLP

Extract disease terms, symptoms, formulations and references from digitized Sanskrit texts.

Clinical Research AI

Assist with phenotype normalization, data cleaning and research workflows—not autonomous diagnosis.

Example research question: Can the classical description of “one object perceived as two” be systematically characterized using modern monocular/binocular diplopia testing?
